> free project and invoice tracker for freelancers, and I added a help chat. I
> need real people to try it, so please be honest about what doesn't work.

> 1. Sign up at https://www.freeflow.website and add a project or two.
> 2. Open the chat at https://www.freeflow.website/chat.
> 3. Ask it at least three questions you would really have, in your own words.
> 4. Ask it one thing you think it shouldn't be able to answer.
> 5. Rate the answers with the thumbs, and send a ticket if it can't help.
> 6. Then tell me: what were you trying to do, did it work, where did it get
>    something wrong, and what would you change?

> view on whether that holds up. Please use made-up client details and no real
> passwords.
