Memory + flying: three ways to combine them

Each one keeps the one-button flying from Fly the week and adds the "can you keep it all in your head?" of Remember. Throwaway sketches: tap, click or press space to climb. You have three hearts in each.

1 · Answer gates

New jobs pop up while you fly. Every few stacks a gate with three openings asks about one of them, and you answer by flying through the right opening.

  • Clients move deadlines mid-flight (amber messages). The gates ask about the new date.
  • A wrong opening or a stack costs a heart.
  • Score = days flown + 3 per right answer. Easy to compare, so it suits a leaderboard later.

Feeling: flying and remembering at the same time is genuinely hectic, which is the point.

2 · Fly, then recall

Fly a week (five stacks) while jobs and changes pop up. Then the game pauses for a two-question memory check before the next week.

  • Calmer than gates: flying and remembering take turns.
  • Each right answer earns FreeFlow reminders, so the next week's stacks are wider.
  • Shows the product's value as a reward for paying attention.

Feeling: a rhythm of tension and breather.

3 · Your head vs FreeFlow

The answer-gate game, played twice on exactly the same course. First from memory. Then again with FreeFlow's list above the game, updating as clients change things.

  • The retry is the product demo: same week, but now you can look it up.
  • The ending puts the two runs side by side.
  • Takes two short runs instead of one.

Feeling: "oh, that's what it's like to have it written down."
