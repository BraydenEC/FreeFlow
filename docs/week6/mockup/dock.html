The Dock replaces the crowded top bar

One small button rests at the bottom centre of every page. Hover it, or tap it on a phone, and it opens into every page, grouped. Icons grow as the pointer passes, a label shows above the one you point at, and a dot marks where you are. Sign in and account stay in the top-right corner, outside the Dock.
