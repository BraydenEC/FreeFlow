## 1. The story

> The story is needing to build a class website, I thought about something that would have been useful for me and how I could make my life and the life of others around me easier, my friends and others, having done freelance work I think its something I wish I had access to. The brand purpose is to bring ease to freelancers, in my peronal opinion having had to actually build projects on my own and do it for other people was already difficult and stressful, beyond this I think that having a helper, personal assistant that can help you make this step less of an issue and reduce friction is important and meaningful for me. Having had gone through it, I want others to understand that I have been through it, and want to listen in order to make the best possible app for everyone. I want them to think that its an app that will surve a purpose for them, something that is made to be catered towards them and to be of actual use. I want people to think of this to be of surplus value in their workflow and be a "necessity" if even. I dont know the pricing yet for the fututure. I have notes from friends who do their own freelance work and one of them has said that if the app was genuinely updated consustently with differet features, fixes and so on then they would use it because it is genuinely useful to have a tool like this that makes there workflow easy with reduced friction. The demo lives within the website i will let the design of it be left for later.
